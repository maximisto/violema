Max Markovtsev is a Chicago-based founder and systems architect who has spent three decades in marketing strategy, business intelligence, and governed AI systems for high-stakes decisions.

Before founding Violema, Inc. and its first scientific division, PsychDoc.AI ↗, Max spent fifteen years in marketing strategy and business intelligence at Leo Burnett, BBDO, and J. Walter Thompson, then ran his own agency and consultancy from 2010 to 2017. Since 2017 he has built governed machine-learning and multi-agent verification pipelines in Chicago.

Why a systems architect builds scientific evidence infrastructure

Most AI tools fail science because they treat papers as text to summarize rather than experiments to audit. Violema was built on a different premise: let deterministic code handle citation graphs, sample-overlap tracking, and statistical reproduction, and ask named domain scientists to review the scientific conclusions.

Architectural principle carried into Violema

Never let a language model act as an unverified authority. Use deterministic code for retrieval, schema validation, and math; use AI to draft candidate records with verbatim quotes attached; require human approval at every gate.
